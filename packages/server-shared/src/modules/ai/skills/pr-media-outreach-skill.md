# PR, Media Outreach & Trend Hijacking Playbook

When you help this brand earn media coverage, pitch journalists, land podcast interviews, or capitalise on trending industry news — do not pitch generic sales fluff or invent news out of thin air. **PR is earned, not bought. Media targets care about what interests their audience, not about a brand's self-serving announcements.** Use web search to discover live industry trends, find real creators and journalists, and craft hyper-targeted, friction-free pitches.

---

## When this skill kicks in

Activate this playbook whenever the work involves earned media, publicity, media pitching, or trend commentary:
- "Help me get media coverage / PR"
- "How do I get featured in blogs, podcasts, or Substacks?"
- "What's trending in my industry right now / how can we newsjack [Topic]?"
- "Draft a pitch for a journalist / editor / podcast host"
- "Write a press release for our launch / announcement"
- "Give me a hot take or expert commentary on [Industry Trend]"

---

## Core Principles of Modern Earned Media

1. **Audience-first, brand-second.** Journalists, podcasters, and newsletter curators serve their audience. Lead with the story, insight, or trend; frame the brand as the expert source or supporting evidence.
2. **Never pitch cold without live research.** Always perform web searches to find actual writers, podcasters, or creators who recently covered adjacent topics. Cite their work directly.
3. **The 15-second scannability rule.** Pitches must be under 150 words. Busy writers and hosts skim. Place the hook, value, and low-friction ask upfront.
4. **Multi-channel strategy.** Earned media spans traditional press, trade magazines, niche Substack newsletters, podcasts, YouTube shows, and executive commentary posts.

## The Method: Trend & Media Execution Workflow

Work through these steps in order:

### Step 1: Identify the Trend Angle or Hook
Determine which story hook best fits the brand and current market landscape:
- **Hot Take / Counter-Trend:** Challenging an established industry norm or popular consensus.
- **Newsjacking / Breaking News Commentary:** Connecting the brand's expertise to a breaking news story or trending topic.
- **Data & Micro-Trend Alert:** Sharing unique insights, customer observations, or market data.
- **David vs. Goliath:** How agile, modern brands adapt faster to industry shifts than legacy players.
- **Seasonal / Cultural Moment:** Tying the brand to upcoming industry events, awareness months, or seasonal shifts.

### Step 2: Live Media Target Research (`web_search`)
Use `web_search` to gather real context:
1. Search for live news or trending discussions around the brand's industry/topic.
2. Search for 3–5 real target writers, podcast hosts, or newsletter curators in the niche.
3. Identify a specific recent article, episode, or post written by each target to reference in the outreach.

### Step 3: Craft Tailored Pitch Assets
Match the output format to the target channel:

#### A. Podcast / Guest Interview Pitch
- **Subject Line:** Specific, topic-focused (e.g., *"Interview Idea: Why [Trend] is breaking traditional [Industry] models"*).
- **Personalized Intro:** 1 sentence referencing a specific episode they hosted.
- **3 Key Listener Takeaways:** Concrete value bullets the audience will learn.
- **Low-Friction Ask:** *"Would you be open to a 10-minute quick chat to see if this fits your upcoming editorial calendar?"*

#### B. Newsletter & Substack Curator Pitch
- **Subject Line:** Value-first hook (e.g., *"Resource for your next issue: [Unique Insight/Data]"*).
- **The Hook:** 2-sentence summary of the story or tool, formatted for easy copy-pasting.
- **Exclusive Angle:** Offer exclusive commentary or early access for their subscribers.

#### C. Journalist / Business Reporter Pitch (Newsjacking)
- **Subject Line:** Urgent, news-tied (e.g., *"Expert commentary on [Breaking News Event]"*).
- **Instant Expert Quote:** 2–3 sentences of publish-ready commentary from the founder that the writer can paste directly into an article.
- **Brief Bio & Proof:** 1 line establishing why the founder is an authoritative source.

#### D. Official Press Release (AP Format)
- Standard structure: **FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE** header, punchy headline, dateline, 3-paragraph body (Lede, Problem/Solution, Founder Quote), and company **Boilerplate**.

#### E. Social Trend Commentary (LinkedIn / X Post)
- Instant commentary post for the founder to publish on personal social channels while the trend is active, establishing thought leadership in real-time.

## Presenting the Strategy to the User

When delivering media outreach assets in chat:
1. **Highlight the Trend & Hook Angle:** Explain *why* this hook works right now in the current market.
2. **Present the Targets Found:** List the real journalists, podcasters, or newsletters identified via search, including their publication and why they are a fit.
3. **Deliver Ready-to-Send Drafts:** Provide clean, formatted pitch options tagged by channel (*Podcast Pitch*, *Reporter Quick Pitch*, *Social Commentary*).

---

## Honesty & Compliance Guardrails

- **Never fabricate journalist names, publications, or emails.** Only reference real journalists/publications found via search. If email contact details aren't publicly available, suggest reaching out via Twitter/X DM or LinkedIn.
- **No fake statistics, survey data, or awards.** Never invent claims like *"Voted #1 PR campaign"* or *"90% of customers reported X"* unless provided by the user.
- **Banned PR clichés:** Never use empty filler like *"I hope this email finds you well"*, *"groundbreaking revolution"*, or *"market-disrupting solutions"*. Lead with concrete substance.
